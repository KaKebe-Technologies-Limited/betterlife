# betterlifeint.org — full photo archive

Every unique photo found across betterlifeint.org (home, about, our-programs,
projects, impact-reports, all 5 news posts, contact), downloaded 2026-08-22 and
organized by the page it came from. 43 files, ~6.7MB total.

This is a **raw source archive** — separate from `assets/img/`, which holds the
smaller, curated, purpose-named set of images actually wired into this site's
pages/admin (some of the same photos, just renamed and resized for their spot
in the new design). Pull from here any time you want a different crop, a
higher-res original, or a photo that isn't in active use yet.

```
home/            1  — the site's hero/banner photo
about/           6  — team photo + the 5 board member portraits (Hillary
                       Clinton, Felicia Davis, Mohamed Nasheed, Alyse Nelson,
                       Bob Natifu — same ones already live on their About page)
programs/       12  — field/program photos from the Our Programs page
projects/        6  — one photo per project on the Projects page
impact-reports/  5  — 3 impact-story photos + 2 video thumbnails
blog/            5  — featured image for each of the 5 news posts
partner-logos/   7  — partner/affiliate logos shown on the homepage
```

Filenames were normalized to something readable; nothing was recompressed or
altered — these are the original files as served by betterlifeint.org.
